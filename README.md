# Urban Mobility & Economic Productivity in Latin American Cities

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes the relationship between **urban mobility and economic productivity** in major Latin American cities.

Using real-world data from the **TomTom Traffic Index** and **OECD Cities**, the project explores traffic congestion and economic indicators to identify patterns and relationships between transportation conditions and economic productivity.

The analysis provides data-driven insights that can support the evaluation of urban transportation infrastructure.

##  Objectives

- Clean and prepare mobility and economic datasets.
- Combine data from TomTom Traffic Index and OECD Cities.
- Analyze traffic congestion and economic productivity across cities.
- Identify patterns and relationships between mobility and economic indicators.
- Create visualizations to communicate the findings.

##  Data Sources

- **TomTom Traffic Index** — urban traffic and congestion indicators.
- **OECD Cities** — socioeconomic and economic indicators for cities.

##  Limitations

The analysis identifies relationships between variables but does not establish causation. Other factors, such as population density, infrastructure, industry composition, and labor markets, may also influence economic productivity.
